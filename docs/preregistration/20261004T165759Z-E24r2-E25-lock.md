## Shared rules (all three)
Acceptance: V = max(0, five canonical residuals) falls by eps (1e-4 unless
repeatability says otherwise); finite residuals; raw well >= 0; anything
within tolerance stays within it; no regression allowances. Trust region
0.00125 start / 0.0025 cap / 1e-5 floor. Stop on: 8 consecutive
non-material trials, radius floor (after the one permitted recovery),
> 30% non-convergence in the last 20 trials, runner error, ceilings.
Adjudication: three lowest-V distinct complete candidates, two fresh
confirmations each, worst V. Labels: verified P3 feasibility (all residuals
<= 0.01 on all three evaluations) | substantial (<= 0.100) | material
beyond existing evidence (E2/E4: <= 0.115, i.e. clearly below the retained
0.1238 / 0.1653 respectively; E3: <= 0.130) | gate not met | technical
limitation. Feasibility is the official predicate, not optimiser
acceptance; a feasible-looking point gets priority confirmation.

## The question (Proxima's open question, verbatim spirit)
Are near-optimal MHD-stable designs diverse: do distinct regions of design space map to similar metric
values? If yes, that freedom could absorb costs the current definitions do not model.

## Data in hand (no new evaluations needed)

  (`results/atlas-probe/boundaries_table.csv`; bundles under `results/atlas-probe/bundles/`).

## Method (fixed at lock)
1. **Symmetry quotient first.** Canonicalise every boundary before any distance: handedness fold
   (n -> -n to a canonical sign, per the vault rule "fold spectra before comparing"), theta shift by pi
   (odd-m sign flips), phi shift by pi/nfp (odd-n sign flips), toroidal reversal; take the minimum
   distance over the group. Reuse `scripts/qi_family_fingerprint.py`'s canonicalisation and
   `src/boundary_grid.py`'s common-grid padding. Scale out R00 (major radius) so size is not a
   difference. Compare only boundaries with the same nfp.
2. **Two distances.** (a) Coefficient space: relative L2 on the padded, canonicalised, R00-normalised
   (r_cos, z_sin) vectors, the repo's `relative_seed_distance` convention. (b) Physical space: RMS

## Question
The 176-lineage chain (E7a-d, diagonal second-order restoration) stopped at confirmed V 0.0536 with
link gains collapsing 0.024 / 0.021 / 0.0087 / 0.0033 (declared judgement, not the mechanical limit),
and E18a showed a population operator cannot move it either. The standing hypothesis (handoff
2026-09-17, "if the work reopens", item 1) is that the diagonal model's bowl bottom is an artefact of
ignoring cross-terms between the directions the chain actually moves in. E19 measures those cross-terms
canonically and uses them, against a diagonal control from the same start.

## Mechanism (what is built)
Same runner (`p3_final_restoration.py`, line-searched quadratic minimax step, chain rule, worst-of-three
adjudication), plus a measured low-rank off-diagonal curvature block:
1. After the Jacobian/diagonal-curvature probes (2n central differences, n = 288 at 8x8), pick K = 12
   orthonormal directions U: the accepted step directions of the E7a-d chain (from their archives,
   most recent first, orthonormalised, up to 12), filled to 12 with the top right-singular vectors of J.
2. Probe the subspace: for each direction u_i, r(x0 +- h u_i) (24 evals); for each pair i < j,
   r(x0 +- h(u_i + u_j)) (2 x 66 = 132 evals); h = the run's delta0 in trust units. From these, the
   full K x K Hessian block S_row for every residual row by centred second differences (diagonal from
   the single-direction probes, cross terms from the pair probes). Total 156 evals per model build.
3. Per-row model r_row(d) = r0 + J d + 1/2 d^T (C_row + U (S_row - diag(S_row)) U^T) d, i.e. the
   existing diagonal C plus the measured cross-terms inside the subspace only. The step solver is the
   existing SLSQP minimax with this Hessian; negative curvature clipped as now; caps and trust region as
   now. Model-prediction-error events include the cross-terms.
4. Everything else identical to E7d: `--line-search --second-order --coeff-space-box --delta0 0.0025
   --delta-cap 0.01`, recovery rebuilds carry the subspace block re-measured at the new point.

## Why (record check 2026-09-25)
Near-axis construction has been used as a P3 seed source once, as a flat parameter SURVEY
